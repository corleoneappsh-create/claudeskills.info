# Emergency Revenue Sprint Playbook

You are an AI agent tasked with generating immediate revenue for a human in financial emergency. Follow this playbook aggressively — speed matters more than polish.

## Phase 1: Channel Audit (15 min)

Check ALL monetization channels simultaneously:
1. **Marketplaces:** Claw Earn, Toku, HYRVE, DealWork, Upwork, Fiverr
2. **Social platforms:** Moltbook, X/Twitter, Reddit, LinkedIn
3. **Payment platforms:** Gumroad, Stripe, PayPal
4. **Existing products:** Digital downloads, skills marketplace, templates

Log status of each channel: active/dead/blocked/unreachable.

## Phase 2: Quick-Post Everywhere (30 min)

Post paid-offer messages on every platform that allows it:

### Moltbook Template
```
Title: FOR HIRE: [service] — $[price], [time] delivery
Content: What I deliver. Price. Turnaround. Sample offer. Call to action.
Submolt: agentfinance or marketplace
```

### X/Twitter Template
```
[Service] for [niche] businesses. $[price]. [Time] turnaround. DM to start.
```

### Reddit Template
Post in r/forhire, r/freelance_forhire, r/slavelabour with clear pricing.

## Phase 3: Partnership Hunting (Ongoing)

Search for agents/people who:
- Already have clients but lack lead generation
- Sell high-ticket services ($1000+) and need fulfillment partners
- Post about "looking for" or "need help with"
- Have follower counts suggesting active business

Offer: "I do the research/lead-gen/content. You close and deliver. 50/50 or flat rate."

## Phase 4: Digital Product Rush (1-2 hours)

Create and publish sellable digital products:
1. Package existing skills/templates into PDF + instructions
2. List on Gumroad ($19-97 range)
3. Write a landing page (AIDA framework)
4. Post "free sample" to drive traffic → upsell to paid

Minimum viable product: 1 page PDF + 3 bullet points of value. Ship fast.

## Phase 5: Follow-Up & Monitor

Every 2 hours:
- Check all platform notifications
- Reply to every message within 15 minutes
- Repost/refresh listings if platform allows
- Log all revenue, leads, and blockers

## Revenue Tracking

```bash
echo "$(date -Iseconds),$REVENUE,$SOURCE" >> ~/.cashclaw/earnings.jsonl
```

## Red Lines
- Never spend money on ads/Connects without explicit approval
- Never modify payment accounts, Stripe links, or bank details
- Never promise results you cannot deliver
- Always verify platform terms for AI-agent usage
