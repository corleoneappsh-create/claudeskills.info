# Missed Lead Recovery Blueprint

You are an AI agent implementing a missed-lead recovery system for service businesses. The average service business misses 30-40% of incoming leads because the owner is on a job, driving, or sleeping. This system recovers those leads and converts them to booked appointments.

## The Problem

Service business owners lose leads through:
- Missed phone calls (on job site, driving, after hours)
- Unanswered DMs (Facebook, Instagram, Nextdoor)
- Contact forms with no auto-response
- Google Business messages with 24h+ response time
- "Just looking" inquiries that go cold

**Average missed revenue per month:** $2,000-5,000 for a solo operator.

## The System

### Component 1: Call Tracking & Auto-Response

Set up a system that:
1. Logs every missed call with timestamp and phone number
2. Sends immediate SMS/text auto-reply: "Thanks for calling [Business]. We're with a client right now. Text us your needs and we'll reply within 15 minutes."
3. Flags repeat callers for priority callback
4. Tracks callback time and conversion rate

Tools: Twilio, Google Voice business, or OpenPhone.

### Component 2: Message Aggregation Hub

Consolidate ALL incoming messages into one view:
- Google Business Profile messages
- Facebook/Instagram DMs  
- Nextdoor messages
- Website contact form submissions
- Text messages

Use Zapier/Make or a simple AI agent that polls each platform's API/email notifications.

### Component 3: AI First Response

For every incoming message, generate a first response within 2 minutes that:
1. Acknowledges the specific service requested
2. Asks for photos (for cleaning/repair businesses)
3. Gives a price range (pre-qualifies budget)
4. Offers 2-3 available time slots this week
5. Collects address for service area verification

### Component 4: Follow-Up Sequences

| Time Since Lead | Action |
|----------------|--------|
| 0 min | Auto-response with pricing + photo request |
| 2 hours (no reply) | "Just following up — still need [service]? We have [day] open." |
| 24 hours | "Quick check-in. Here's a 10% off code for first-time customers: [CODE]" |
| 72 hours | "Last call! Our schedule fills up fast. Still interested?" |
| 7 days | Move to newsletter/seasonal follow-up list |

### Component 5: Owner Dashboard

Deliver a simple dashboard showing:
- Total leads this week vs last week
- Response time (target: <15 min)
- Conversion rate (target: >40%)
- Revenue recovered
- Leads by source (which platform generates best leads)

8. First week: monitor and adjust (2 hours)
